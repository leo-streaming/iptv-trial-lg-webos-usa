## 1. What Is an IPTV Trial for LG webOS?

An IPTV trial is a temporary opportunity to evaluate an internet-based television service before choosing a paid subscription.

**IPTV** stands for Internet Protocol Television. Instead of relying exclusively on cable or satellite delivery, IPTV uses an internet connection to deliver television streams to compatible playback devices.

**LG webOS** is the smart TV operating system used on many LG televisions. It provides an interface for installing supported applications, managing streaming services, and navigating entertainment content with an LG remote control.

An IPTV trial for LG webOS therefore involves two separate requirements:

1. A compatible IPTV player must be available and functional on the television.
2. The viewing service must provide usable account access for that player.

A successful trial requires both. Having an active subscription does not automatically make an incompatible application work.

### IPTV Player vs. IPTV Subscription

An **IPTV player** is the software interface used to open and navigate supported television streams, playlists, and program information.

An **IPTV subscription** supplies access to the viewing service, subject to its terms and content permissions.

Some applications charge separately for their software license or premium features. Others are available without a separate player purchase.

The app developer and the viewing supplier may be different businesses. Buyers should understand which company is responsible for each payment and support request.

### What Is a Consumer Playback Trial?

A consumer playback trial is intended to test personal viewing access, streaming functionality, and device setup.

It is different from a reseller-panel demonstration, which concerns commercial account administration, subscriber management, or reseller operations.

The TereaTV trial discussed here is a consumer playback trial, not a reseller-panel demo.

## 3. Why LG Model Year Matters Before Choosing IPTV

The model year is an important compatibility clue because LG televisions have used different webOS generations over time.

However, **model year, webOS version, and firmware version are not interchangeable**.

A newer television may support software features that an older model cannot use. Individual applications may also impose their own minimum operating-system requirements.

### Understanding the Three Compatibility Details

**LG model number:** The identification code associated with your exact television model.

**Model year:** The year or model generation associated with the television. This is not necessarily the year you purchased it.

**webOS version:** The operating-system generation installed on your television.

**Firmware version:** The installed software build for your television. Firmware updates may fix issues, but they do not guarantee compatibility with every new app.

### LG webOS Generations to Recognize

Software upgrades and differences between models can affect the installed version.
